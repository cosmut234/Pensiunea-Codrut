Frequently asked questions

Where is Pensiunea Codrut?

At Strada Principală nr. 418B, Moieciu de Jos, Brașov County, Romania, in the Bran–Moieciu area.

How far is Bran Castle?

The driving route is approximately 5.4 km and usually takes about 9 minutes by car, depending on traffic.

Can I book directly?

Yes. Send your dates and number of guests by WhatsApp, call +40 742 599 860 or email pensiuneacodrut@yahoo.com.

Is the guesthouse suitable for a group?

Yes. The entire 11-room property can be booked for 22 adults and 6 children, for a minimum stay of two nights.

What facilities are available?

Free Wi-Fi, private parking, an equipped shared kitchen, outdoor hot tub, barbecue area, games room, covered terrace and children's playground.
